About Me

I'm Vicky Kumar, a second-year B.Tech student in Computer Science (AI & ML) at Rungta International Skills University (RISU'29). I build full-stack web apps and enjoy turning real problems into working software.

I work with C++, Java, Python and JavaScript, and I build with React, Node.js and MongoDB. I practise data structures and algorithms regularly. I am an ICPC Amritapuri Student Ambassador and a 2nd Runner-up at HackStormXRIU.

My projects cover a food nutrition guide, a GPS-based PG finder, a coding streak tracker and a civic complaints platform. I learn by building, I join hackathons, and I want to contribute to open source. I care about clean code, finishing what I start, and getting a little better every day.
